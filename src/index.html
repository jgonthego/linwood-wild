    Data: iNaturalist API v1, research-grade observations only, within ~{{RADIUS}} km of the Linwood, NJ town centre. Pulled {{DATE}}. Counts = number of research-grade observations (all years); month bars = observations per calendar month.

    Mainland only: every observation's coordinates were checked against OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). Records on the barrier islands (Margate, Longport, Ocean City…), on bay or open water, with privacy-obscured locations, or with GPS uncertainty over 1 km were dropped ({{DROPPED}} observations). Ocean-only animals (whales, dolphins, seals, sea turtles, gannets, scoters, eiders, loons, pelicans, shearwaters and the like) are excluded unless a record sits on mainland land here. Records on the salt-marsh edge are kept; birds found mostly there are filed under "Flyovers & nearby marsh".

    Photos: from iNaturalist, shown only when licensed CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA or CC BY-NC; each card shows its photographer credit and license. Species with no open-licensed photo show a placeholder.

    Sounds: {{SOUNDS}} bird species have a recording — one clip each from a research-grade iNaturalist observation with a CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA or CC BY-NC sound license, preferring recordings made within 15 km of Linwood, then New Jersey, then the eastern US. Clips are trimmed to at most 15 seconds and re-encoded as mono MP3; the recordist, license and source observation are shown with every player. "Song"/"Call" labels appear only when the recordist tagged it in a structured iNaturalist field (free-text notes are not used). xeno-canto was not used (its API requires an account key).

    Fun facts: first lines of the species' Wikipedia article, as provided through the iNaturalist API (Wikipedia text is CC BY-SA).

    Not a field guide or checklist authority — just what people have photographed and the iNat community has confirmed. Fonts: Anton & Space Grotesk (SIL OFL).
