    How the list is built: every species with a wildlife record since 2000 in GBIF (which bundles eBird checklists, iNaturalist research-grade observations and museum specimens) within about 15 km of Linwood, mainland only — the barrier islands and ocean are cut off. Each species is rated by how many records sit inside three rings: 2 km, 8 km and 15 km. Data pulled {{DATE}}.

    Birds: {{RULE_BIRDS}}. Everything else: {{RULE_OTHER}}. Bird seasons and month bars use eBird checklists only: the share of all eBird bird records per month within 8 km, which evens out the fact that more people go birding in some months. Other groups show raw records per month within 15 km.

    Left out: ocean-only animals (whales, dolphins, seals, sea turtles, gannets, shearwaters, storm-petrels, jaegers, alcids, loons, scoters, eiders, pelicans and similar) — {{REMOVED}} species dropped by that rule. Names that couldn't be matched to an iNaturalist species ({{UNMATCHED}}) are also left out. Coverage limits: birds are very well covered thanks to eBird. Insects, spiders and herps depend on what people happened to photograph, so the real number of bugs is far higher than shown — mostly butterflies, moths, dragonflies, bees and beetles make the list.

    "Logged near here": research-grade iNaturalist sightings within about 5 km on the mainland (separate count, from an earlier pull).

    Photos: from iNaturalist, only when licensed CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA or CC BY-NC; each card shows the photographer credit and license. Sounds: {{SOUNDS}} birds have one clip each from a research-grade iNaturalist observation with a CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA / CC BY-NC sound license, preferring recordings from near Linwood, then New Jersey, then the eastern US; clips are trimmed to 15 seconds or less and re-encoded as mono MP3, credited next to each player. xeno-canto wasn't used because its API needs an account key. Photo galleries: tap any photo. Bird photos labeled Male / Female / Juvenile come only from research-grade iNaturalist observations that carry that Sex or Life Stage annotation (open licences only, credited on every photo); {{MF}} of {{BIRDS}} birds have both male- and female-labeled photos. Extra photos are the species' curated iNaturalist photos, unlabeled. "How to tell male vs female" notes are verbatim sentences from each bird's Wikipedia article (CC BY-SA), shown for {{SEXNOTES}} birds. Describe-a-Bird traits: a hand-curated field-guide trait table (colors, size, markings, behavior, habitat) for {{TRAITS}} birds, cross-checked against Wikipedia — a helper, not an authority. Journal: stored only in your browser (localStorage + IndexedDB); this site has no server and never receives it. Fun facts: the first lines of each species' Wikipedia article, via the iNaturalist API (CC BY-SA).
